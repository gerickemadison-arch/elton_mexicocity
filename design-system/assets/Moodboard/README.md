# Moodboard

These are the reference images the system was built from. They're for mood only and must not be published: most are other people's work.

- **The 1975 Walk of Fame suit** (not uploaded; shared in conversation) is the primary source. Pistachio satin became the satin band, the bottle-green lapel became the `lapel` ground, the gold stars became `gold`, and the pink star glasses became `pink`.
- **poster-msg-1989**: star rows and tight billing.
- **star-dancer-lettering**: green and gold together, and the hanging star.
- **step-right-up-sunburst**: the sunburst and the notched ticket frame.
- **green-stripe-save-the-date**: striped band with a scalloped edge.
- **pinwheel-record-suite**, **one-night-only-event**, **popcorn-paddles**: the carnival energy, used sparingly (the sunburst only).
- **ribbon-invitation**, **weekend-events-card**, **handwritten-note**: event-name hierarchy and itinerary layout.
