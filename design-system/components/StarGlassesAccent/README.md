# Star Glasses accent

How to use the hot-pink Star Glasses theme as a side accent inside the Lapel system: switch on `data-theme="star-glasses"` for one panel or one small piece, and every role token remaps to pink.

**Where it goes**
- **One side panel** on a Lapel piece, at most one third of it. The after-party column on the night's schedule is the model.
- **Small standalone pieces** that belong to the late-night moment: drink tickets, after-party cards, wristbands, stickers and coasters.

**Rules**
- Lapel always leads. Pink covers no more than about 25% of any piece, and the invitation and itinerary front stay fully Lapel.
- Use one pink panel per piece. Never put two pink panels side by side, and never set a pink panel inside another pink area.
- The panel uses the same components and tokens. Only the theme changes: deep-green text and labels, berry for the aside, tangerine stripes.
- No sunburst in pink. The sunburst belongs to the concert, which stays Lapel.
- The Lapel `spark` (the pink italic line) still counts as that piece's single pink line, so a piece with a pink panel drops its pink aside on the green side.

**How**: put `data-theme="star-glasses"` on the panel's container. The tokens (`ground`, `text`, `label`, `stripe`, `spark` and the rest) remap inside it.
